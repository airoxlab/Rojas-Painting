# Rojas Painting

Single-page website for Rojas Painting — an owner-operated painting contractor based in
North Natomas, Sacramento, serving Sacramento, Natomas, Elk Grove and Roseville.

- **Phone:** (916) 813-0097
- **Based:** North Natomas, Sacramento, CA 95834
- **Status:** Licensed California painting contractor (Aug 2026), bonded, sole owner, no employees

Services: interior painting, exterior and stucco, cabinets and built-ins, rentals and move-out
turnovers. Free written estimates.

## Structure

- `index.html` - the complete site (self-contained CSS, no build step)
- `img/` - 8 verified stock photographs of residential painting work, credited in the footer

Nine sections: hero, services, local-to-Sacramento, how it works, why us, the work, areas served,
free estimate, contact. Mobile-first, dark-mode aware.

## Sourcing notes

Brand-new business with no online presence - no website, no Google profile, no social. All photos
are verified stock, disclosed in the footer as representative rather than completed projects.
No license number, owner name, review count, or years in business appears anywhere, since none
could be verified. (A reverse lookup suggests the owner may be Raul Rojas, but this is unconfirmed
and deliberately omitted.)

**Name collision:** several unrelated Rojas Painting businesses exist - Rojas Painting Inc of
Rohnert Park (Oscar Rojas, founded 2018, Sonoma/Marin, 69 Yelp reviews, website + Instagram, and
which lists a Sacramento registered-agent address in some directories), plus others in Napa and
Hayward. The site turns this into a selling point with a dedicated "There are a few Rojas
Paintings. This is the Sacramento one." section, and the footer states the non-affiliation
explicitly.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or deploy the folder as-is to any static host.
